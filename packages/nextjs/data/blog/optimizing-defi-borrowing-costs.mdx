# Introduction

Lending rates change, and a position that was inexpensive to borrow against can become costly. Kapan Finance helps you compare markets and refinance supported positions between lending protocols. Flash loans can supply the temporary funds needed to repay the old debt and establish the new position in one transaction. Whether a move saves money depends on the rates, transaction costs and how long you keep the position.

# The DeFi Lending Landscape and Loan Migration

Decentralized lending protocols offer different interest rates and risk models. Platforms like Aave and Compound adjust rates based on supply and demand. Moving a supported position between markets—often called **DeFi loan migration** or **flash loan debt migration**—can reduce borrowing costs when the expected interest savings outweigh the cost of the move.

## Why Rates Vary and the Opportunity for Migration

Interest rates across DeFi platforms differ due to:
- **Supply and Demand Dynamics:** High borrowing demand against limited supply drives rates up, and vice versa.
- **Protocol Models:**
  - **Aave** employs a “rate slope” system, where rates rise sharply as utilization increases.
  - **Compound** uses a similar yet uniquely parameterized approach.
- **Market Factors:** Risk premiums, liquidation parameters, and collateral requirements also play a significant role.

These differences make refinancing worth comparing, along with each market’s collateral requirements and liquidation thresholds.

- **Protocol Dashboards:** Both Aave and Compound offer real-time insights into current rates.
- **Aggregator Tools:** Platforms like DeFiLlama and DeFi Pulse consolidate data from multiple protocols.
- **Kapan Finance:** Compare available lending markets and review refinancing options for your supported positions.

# The Power of Flash Loans in Debt Migration

Kapan uses flash loans for supported **debt migration** flows. A flash loan supplies funds to repay the existing debt, freeing collateral to move to the destination market. The new borrowing then repays the flash loan within the same transaction.

- **Atomic Execution:** The migration steps complete together or revert. Token approvals and protocol permissions may require separate confirmations beforehand.
- **Temporary Funding:** A supported route can fund repayment without requiring you to hold the full debt amount in your wallet.
- **Your Rate Decision:** You choose whether and when to refinance. Review gas, any flash-loan fees, swap costs and the destination rate before signing.

# The Pitfalls of Traditional Debt Unwinding

Traditional debt unwinding methods can be complex, time-consuming, and fraught with inefficiencies. Consider these common challenges:

- **Funds Used Elsewhere:** Borrowed assets may already be deployed in another position. Sourcing repayment funds can require selling or withdrawing those assets.
- **Multiple Transaction Steps:** A manual move may require:
  - Repaying your existing debt on the current platform.
  - Withdrawing and depositing collateral into the destination market.
  - Borrowing again at the destination.
  Each step can incur gas costs, and any asset swaps can add fees and slippage.
- **Different Chains:** Kapan’s refinancing flows move supported positions on the same chain. Bridging wallet assets is a separate action, not cross-chain debt migration.
- **Repayment Funding:** Flash loans can cover the existing debt in supported routes, subject to liquidity and the destination market’s borrowing limits.
- **Overall Inconvenience:** These traditional methods disrupt your liquidity position and can lead to a less efficient borrowing strategy. The need to unwind and rebuild positions introduces delays and complexity that can offset potential savings from lower interest rates.


# Step-by-Step Guide to Effective DeFi Loan Migration

Before refinancing a supported position, compare the whole move:

1. **Compare Available Markets:** Check the destination rate, accepted collateral and borrowing limits on your chain.
2. **Estimate Interest Savings:** Compare the rates over the period you expect to keep the position. Variable rates can change after the move.
3. **Include Transaction Costs:** Account for gas, swap costs and any flash-loan fees. Kapan charges no service fee.
4. **Review and Confirm:** Check the proposed route and permissions. After any required approvals, the migration repays the old debt, moves collateral and borrows at the destination to repay the flash loan atomically.
5. **Monitor the New Position:** Keep track of its rate and liquidation threshold as market conditions change.

# Risk Management in Loan Migration

While the benefits of **flash loan debt migration** are compelling, it’s important to be aware of potential risks:

- **Protocol Risk:** Each lending platform has its unique security model and risk profile.
- **Smart Contract Vulnerabilities:** Lending protocols, execution contracts and swap routes can contain bugs.
- **Liquidation Risk:** Different protocols have varying thresholds that can affect your collateral.
- **Market Volatility:** Price swings can influence your collateral requirements and overall strategy.

Review these risks alongside the expected savings. Atomic execution keeps the migration steps together; it does not remove the risks of the resulting position.
